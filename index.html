About Me

Konstantinos Spyrou is a sport scientist with a PhD in Sport Sciences and experience in both university teaching and applied sport science. His work focuses on football and futsal, particularly training load monitoring, physical performance, neuromuscular function, recovery, and injury prevention. He is also interested in the application of research principles, epidemiology, and robust research methods to better understand sport-related questions in real-world settings. Through his research, he aims to contribute to the development of practical, evidence-informed approaches that connect scientific findings with the needs of athletes, coaches, and practitioners. He is currently involved in teaching and research at Nebrija University.
